Teen2Teen 1
Speaking Questions
Units 1–12  •  120 Questions
Unit 1: Welcome to English class.
Grammar: Verb be: affirmative and negative; Subject pronouns
Vocabulary: Greetings: hellos and goodbyes
1.  What is your name? How do you spell it?
2.  Where are you from? Are you from Uzbekistan?
3.  How old are you? Are you a student?
4.  What is your teacher's name? Is she/he nice?
5.  Are you happy in English class? Why or why not?
6.  Who is your best friend? Is he or she in your class?
7.  What is your favorite greeting in English? Do you use it every day?
8.  Are you good at English? What is easy and what is difficult for you?
9.  How do you say hello and goodbye to your friends in Uzbekistan?
10.  Is English class interesting for you? What do you like about it?
Unit 2: Is she your mom?
Grammar: Possessive adjectives; Possessive 's and s'; Verb be: yes/no questions; Questions with Who and What
Vocabulary: The family
1.  Who is in your family? How many people are there?
2.  What is your mother's name? What does she do?
3.  Is your family big or small? Tell me about your brothers and sisters.
4.  Who is your favorite person in your family? Why do you like them?
5.  What is your father's job? Is he busy every day?
6.  Do you have grandparents? What are their names?
7.  Is your family home big or small? Who lives there with you?
8.  What does your family do together on weekends?
9.  Who is the funniest person in your family?
10.  What is something special or unique about your family?
Unit 3: Where are you from?
Grammar: Verb be: questions with Where; be in for location; be from for origin
Vocabulary: Countries, nationalities, and hometowns
1.  Where are you from? What is your hometown like?
2.  What country are you from? What is your nationality?
3.  Where is your school? Is it near your home?
4.  What is the most famous city in Uzbekistan? Why is it famous?
5.  Are there any students from other countries in your school?
6.  Where are your favorite singers or actors from?
7.  What country do you want to visit one day? Why?
8.  Is Tashkent a big city? What can people do there?
9.  Where are you right now — at school or at home? Describe the place.
10.  What do you love most about your hometown or city?
Unit 4: Are we late?
Grammar: Prepositions on and at; Verb be: information questions with What time, When, and What day
Vocabulary: Clock times; School subjects; Days of the week; Expressions of punctuality
1.  What time do you wake up on school days?
2.  What is your favorite school subject? What day do you have it?
3.  Are you usually on time for school or are you sometimes late?
4.  What time does your school start and finish?
5.  What do you do on weekends? What time do you usually get up?
6.  Which days of the week do you like the most? Why?
7.  What time do you eat dinner with your family?
8.  Is it important to be on time? Why or why not?
9.  What subject do you have on Monday mornings?
10.  What time do you usually go to sleep? Is that early or late?
Unit 5: The new girl is very cute!
Grammar: Use of adjectives; Intensifier very; Verb be: information questions with What color; Questions with or
Vocabulary: Adjectives to describe people; Adjectives to describe eyes and hair
1.  Describe yourself — what do you look like? What color is your hair and eyes?
2.  Who is the tallest person in your family? Describe them.
3.  Describe your best friend. What does he or she look like?
4.  What color are your eyes? Are they very dark or light?
5.  Describe your favorite teacher. What do they look like?
6.  Are you tall or short? Is that okay with you or do you want to be different?
7.  Describe someone in your class using adjectives. Don't say their name!
8.  What do you think is the most important — how someone looks or how they act?
9.  Describe a famous person you think is very good-looking.
10.  Is your hair straight or curly? Long or short? Do you like your hair?
Unit 6: Today's my birthday!
Grammar: Verb be: questions with How old; Preposition in for months; Preposition on for dates; Ordinal numbers 1st–31st
Vocabulary: Entertainment events; Months of the year
1.  When is your birthday? What month and date is it?
2.  How old are you? How old are your parents?
3.  What do you usually do on your birthday? Do you have a party?
4.  What is your favorite month of the year? Why?
5.  What entertainment events do you enjoy — concerts, movies, or games?
6.  Is there a special event in your city in summer or winter?
7.  When is your favorite holiday? What do you do on that day?
8.  How do people in Uzbekistan celebrate birthdays?
9.  What is the best birthday gift you have ever received?
10.  What event are you looking forward to this year? When is it?
Unit 7: Here. Use my phone.
Grammar: Prepositions and expressions of position and location; The imperative
Vocabulary: Rooms and furniture at home
1.  Describe your bedroom — what furniture is in it and where is everything?
2.  What rooms are in your home? Which room do you spend the most time in?
3.  Where do you usually do your homework — in your bedroom or another room?
4.  What is on your desk right now? Describe what you see.
5.  Is your room tidy or messy? Do you clean it yourself?
6.  Where is your phone right now? Do you use it a lot at home?
7.  Describe your living room. What furniture does it have?
8.  What is your favorite room at home and why?
9.  Where do you keep your school books and supplies at home?
10.  If you could add one new thing to your bedroom, what would it be and where would you put it?
Unit 8: It's really sunny now!
Grammar: Let's / Let's not
Vocabulary: Good weather and bad weather; Free-time activities
1.  What is the weather like in your city today? Do you like it?
2.  What do you like to do on a sunny day?
3.  What do you do on rainy days when you can't go outside?
4.  What is your favorite free-time activity? Do you do it alone or with friends?
5.  What activities do you suggest to your friends when the weather is good?
6.  What is the worst weather you have ever experienced?
7.  Do you prefer hot or cold weather? Why?
8.  What do you usually do after school to relax?
9.  Describe the seasons in your city — which one is the best?
10.  What free-time activity would you like to try but never have?
Unit 9: There's a school next door.
Grammar: there is / there are; Questions with How many
Vocabulary: Expressions of location in the neighborhood; Places in the neighborhood
1.  What places are there in your neighborhood? Describe two or three.
2.  How many schools are there near your home?
3.  Is there a park or playground near your house? Do you go there?
4.  How many students are there in your class?
5.  What shops or stores are there near your school?
6.  Describe your neighborhood — is it quiet or busy?
7.  Is there a market or bazaar near your home? What can you buy there?
8.  How many floors are there in your school building?
9.  What place in your neighborhood do you visit most often?
10.  Is there anything missing in your neighborhood that you wish was there?
Unit 10: Look at those black jeans!
Grammar: Questions with Which; Answers with one / ones; this / that / these / those; Intensifier too
Vocabulary: Clothes; Colors
1.  Describe what you are wearing today. What color are your clothes?
2.  What is your favorite piece of clothing? Why do you love it?
3.  Do you prefer these jeans or those ones? How do you choose your clothes?
4.  What colors do you like to wear? Are there colors you never wear?
5.  Do you like shopping for clothes? Where do you usually buy them?
6.  What do teenagers in Uzbekistan usually wear to school?
7.  Is there a piece of clothing you think is too expensive? What is it?
8.  Describe the clothes your best friend usually wears.
9.  What do you wear in winter to stay warm? And in summer?
10.  If you could buy any piece of clothing right now, what would it be?
Unit 11: I can do that!
Grammar: can / can't for ability; Statements and yes/no questions; Degrees of ability
Vocabulary: Abilities
1.  What can you do very well? Tell me about your best ability.
2.  Can you play a musical instrument? Which one? How well?
3.  What sport can you play? Are you very good at it?
4.  What can't you do but really want to learn?
5.  Can you cook? What dishes can you make?
6.  Can you speak any other languages besides Uzbek and English?
7.  What can your best friend do that you can't?
8.  Can you draw or paint? Do you enjoy art?
9.  What is something you couldn't do before but can do now?
10.  What ability do you think is the most useful in everyday life?
Unit 12: You should visit Brazil!
Grammar: should for advice: statements and yes/no questions; should: information questions
Vocabulary: Reasons to visit a place
1.  What place in Uzbekistan should every tourist visit? Why?
2.  Should students use their phones in class? What do you think?
3.  What should a visitor do on their first day in your city?
4.  What food should tourists definitely try in Uzbekistan?
5.  Should teenagers exercise every day? Why or why not?
6.  What should someone do if they want to learn English faster?
7.  What is one thing students should always do before an exam?
8.  Should you visit a country before you decide to live there? Why?
9.  What advice should you give to a new student joining your school?
10.  What is one thing everyone should do to make the world a better place?
