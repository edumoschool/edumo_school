Exercise-4

PART 1
You are a teacher at a school, and the Head teacher wants to improve the school environment to
make it a better place for both students and staff. The Head teacher has sent out a message
seeking input.
Dear Teaching Staff,
We are committed to making our school an even better place for everyone. To achieve this, we are
looking for innovative ways to enhance our school setting – making it more supportive for students
and more effective for us as educators. We believe your insights are invaluable as you are on the
front lines every day. We have a few initial ideas, but we are eager to hear your thoughts and
suggestions. Your professional experience and perspectives are crucial in shaping a more positive
and productive school environment. Sincerely, The Head teacher

Task 1.1
Write a short message to a colleague, who is your close friend. Tell them about the Head teacher's
plan to improve the school environment. Say what you think about it and what kind of
improvements you hope for. Write about 50 words.
Task 1.2
Write an email to the Head teacher. Express your support for the plan to improve the school
environment. Provide specific suggestions for how the school setting could be made more
supportive for students and more effective for teachers. Write about 120-150 words.
PART 2
You are participating in an online discussion for language learners.
The question is: “What do you think about the impacts of social media on young users?” Post
your response, giving reasons and examples.
Write 180-200 words.

Part-2
Social media has become an integral part of young people's lives, and I perceive that its impacts are
both positive and negative. On the one hand, social media platforms provide young users with
opportunities for connection and communication. They can easily maintain friendships, share
experiences, and engage with diverse communities around the world. For instance, platforms like
Instagram and TikTok allow users to express their creativity and showcase their talents, fostering a
sense of belonging and self- identity.
However, the negative effects of social media cannot be overlooked. One significant concern is the
impact on mental health. Young users often face pressure to present a perfect image online,
leading to feelings of inadequacy and anxiety. Studies have shown that excessive use of social
media can contribute to issues such as depression and low self-esteem. Additionally, exposure to
cyber bullying is a serious problem that can have lasting emotional consequences.
Ultimately, while social media offers valuable opportunities for connection and creativity, it also
poses risks to mental health and well-being. It is essential for young users to navigate these
platforms mindfully, balancing their online interactions with real-world experiences to mitigate
potential negative impacts.
