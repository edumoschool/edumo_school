PART 1
You recently stayed at the Grandview Hotel for a short trip. Unfortunately, your experience was
not great; your room was very noisy, and the air conditioning wasn't working properly. After you
complained upon check-out, the hotel has now sent you an apology letter.
Dear Customer, We truly regret that your experience fell short of our usual high standards. Please be
assured that
we are addressing these issues immediately to prevent similar occurrences for future guests.
As a gesture of our sincere apology, we would like to offer you a complimentary one-night stay in a
newly renovated deluxe room, valid for six months, to provide you with the exceptional experience
we strive for.
We value your feedback and hope you will give us another opportunity to demonstrate our
commitment to guest satisfaction.
Sincerely,
Grandview Hotel Manager

Task 1.1
Write a short message to a friend, who knows about your recent trip. Tell them about the apology
letter you received from the Grandview Hotel. Say what you think about their apology and the
offer. Write about 50 words.
Task 1.2
Write an email to the Hotel Manager. Express your appreciation for the apology and the offer.
Explain if you plan to accept their offer, or if you have an alternative suggestion for a resolution.
Write about 120-150 words.

Part-2
Air pollution is a pressing issue that affects many cities around the world. I ‘m of the opinion that
there’re several key reasons for this problem. Let’s look through some of them!
Firstly, one of the main contributors to air pollution is industrial activity. Factories release a
significant amount of harmful gases and particulate matter into the atmosphere during production
processes. For example, in countries with heavy manufacturing industries, such as China and India,
air quality often suffers due to emissions from factories.
Secondly, transportation is another major factor. Vehicles, especially those that run on fossil fuels
like petrol and diesel, emit carbon monoxide, nitrogen oxides, and other pollutants. In urban areas
with high traffic congestion, these emissions can lead to smog and respiratory issues for residents.
For instance, cities like Los Angeles and Mexico City frequently experience poor air quality due to
the number of cars on the road.
Lastly, household activities also contribute to air pollution. Burning wood or coal for heating and
cooking releases smoke and toxic substances. In many developing countries, this practice is
common and poses health risks to families.
Ultimately, industrial activities, transportation, and household practices are significant reasons for
air pollution, and in my opinion, addressing these issues is crucial for improving air quality and
public health.
