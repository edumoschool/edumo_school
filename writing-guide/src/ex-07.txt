Part-2
This is a really serious and complex question: why is the crime rate among teenagers seemingly
increasing? There isn't one simple answer, but I think several factors are contributing.
One major reason is the influence of social media and technology. Many young people are exposed
to violent content online, which can normalize aggressive behavior. For example, some studies
suggest that teenagers who play violent video games may become desensitized to violence and
more likely to act out in real life.
Furthermore, socio-economic factors play a big role. Poverty, unstable home environments, and
lack of parental supervision can create a breeding ground for crime. When families are struggling,
or when children don't have consistent guidance, they are more vulnerable. For instance, a
teenager from a disadvantaged background might resort to theft out of desperation or a lack of
other options.
Finally, some argue that mental health issues are also a contributing factor. Untreated depression,
anxiety, or other challenges can manifest in destructive ways, including anti-social behaviour.
In short, it's a multifaceted problem that needs a comprehensive approach, focusing on support,
education, and opportunity.
