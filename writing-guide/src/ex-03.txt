PART 1
You are working in an office and your company wants to make new colleagues feel more
welcome. Your company sent out a message about this.
Dear Employee,
We want to make our new colleagues feel very welcome when they start working here! We are
planning a new way to help them settle in quickly and feel part of the team. We are at the
beginning of this plan and want to hear your ideas. We have some initial thoughts below, but we
really want to know what you think.
Your ideas are important to help us make this a great welcome for everyone.
Sincerely,
The HR Department
Task 1.1
Write a short message to a colleague. Tell them about the plan to welcome new staff. Say what
you think about it and what kind of welcome you hope new people will get. Write about 50 words.
Task 1.2
Write an email to the HR Department. Say you are happy about the plan to welcome new staff.
Give some specific ideas for how to make new colleagues feel comfortable and quickly become
part of the team. Write about 120-150 words.

PART 2
You are participating in an online discussion for language learners.
The question is: “Are celebrities good role models for young people?” Post your response, giving

Task-1.2
Dear HR Department,
I am writing in response to your recent message regarding the initiative to enhance the welcome
experience for new colleagues. I wholeheartedly support this plan; fostering a positive and
inclusive onboarding process is crucial for employee retention and overall team morale.
I believe several elements could greatly contribute to making new staff feel comfortable and
integrated quickly. Firstly, implementing a "buddy system" where an experienced employee is
assigned to guide a new starter for their first few weeks could be invaluable. This buddy could
answer informal questions, introduce them to others, and help them navigate company culture.
Secondly, a designated welcome lunch or coffee break within the first week, involving their
immediate team, would provide a relaxed opportunity for introductions. Finally, ensuring that new
employees have all necessary resources and access *before* their first day, and a clear
introductory schedule for their first week, would significantly reduce initial stress and allow them
to focus on learning their role and connecting with colleagues.
Thank you for prioritizing this important aspect of our workplace.
Sincerely, Alex

Part-2
The idea of celebrities as role models for young people is a bit of a mixed bag, in my opinion. While
they certainly have influence, it’s not always for the better.
On one hand, some celebrities are genuinely positive influences. Many use their fame to support
important causes, like raising awareness for environmental issues or promoting educational
charities. For example, an actor who speaks out about the importance of literacy can inspire young
people to value reading. Similarly, athletes who demonstrate dedication and discipline in their
sport can motivate others to pursue their goals with hard work and commitment. These individuals
often showcase admirable qualities like perseverance and resilience.
However, there are significant downsides too. The lifestyles portrayed by many celebrities can be
extremely extravagant and sometimes promote unhealthy values, such as excessive consumerism
or superficial appearances. This can create unrealistic expectations for young fans, leading to
disappointment or a focus on material possessions. Furthermore, some celebrities engage in
controversial behaviour or scandals that are widely publicised, providing a poor example. Young
people, especially, might not always have the maturity to critically assess the information they
receive and could be negatively influenced by these aspects.
Therefore, while celebrities can be role models, it's important for young people to be discerning
and look beyond the glamour. They should focus on the actions and values that truly matter.
