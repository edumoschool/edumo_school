PART 1
You are a member of a fitness club. You received an email from the manager of the club.
Dear Member,
I am sorry to inform you that the fitness center is closing for a month from next Monday.
The building needs some repairs and we also plan to install some new equipment. What
else do you think should be changed? As the center will not be operating for a month,
what kind of alternative activities should we organize in the meantime? We appreciate
your opinion very much.
The Manager.

Task 1.1 Write a letter to your friend, who is also a member of the club. Write about your
feelings and what you think the club management should do about the situation. Write
about 50 words. Task 1.2 Write a letter to the manager. Write about your feelings and
what you think the club management should do about the situation. Write about 120-150
words.

Part-2
In my opinion, both big cities and small towns have their unique advantages, but I believe that
living in a big city offers more opportunities for personal and professional growth.
Firstly, big cities are often hubs of diversity and culture. They provide access to a wide range of
cultural experiences, including museums, theaters, and international cuisine. For example, in cities
like New York or London, one can experience art from various cultures and attend performances
from around the world. This cultural richness can greatly enhance one’s understanding of different
perspectives.
Secondly, big cities typically offer better job prospects. With numerous industries and companies
located in urban areas, individuals have a greater chance of finding employment in their desired
field. For instance, someone interested in technology may find better opportunities in a city like San
Francisco, which is known for its thriving tech scene.
However, it’s important to acknowledge the downsides of city living, such as higher costs of living
and noise. In contrast, small towns often provide a sense of community and tranquility that can be
appealing.
Ultimately, the choice depends on personal preferences and priorities, but for those seeking growth
and adventure, big cities are often the better option.
