# Write it Right! — playful Multilevel writing guide (A4)

A redesign of "A Complete Guide to Multilevel Writing" (src/guide.pdf) in a playful style, with new exercises.

- Front pages: how to use the book, the writing exam (Uzbek), a phrase bank, examiner-marked samples, band descriptors, and a conversion table with a score tracker
- 10 exercises, each with:
  - the situation and the email
  - Task 1.1, Task 1.2 and Part 2
  - Read & think, Word power (matching), and Formal or informal
  - Plan it, and writing pages with checklists
  - model answers with highlighted vocabulary, a linker gap-fill and the word list
- Answer key at the back

Data: data/front.json and data/units/NN.json (format in SCHEMA.md). Cover hero: assets/hero_clean.png.
Build: ./build.sh → dist/Write_it_Right_Multilevel_Writing.pdf
